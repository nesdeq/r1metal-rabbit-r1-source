# Calls

The R1 calls the owner's Signal contacts and groups and takes their calls, with sound alone or with pictures both ways, as a linked device of the owner's account ([Signal](signal.md)). A one-to-one call goes to the far account's devices by ICE, directly or through the relays Signal's service hands out; a group's call goes through Signal's calling server. Screens: [Interface](interface.md#calls). Signal's side, the calls' gate, the log, its syncs, the relays and a group's token: [Signal](signal.md#calls).

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | the contacts a message may go to but the Note to Self, and the groups, as Signal's thread lists them for the screens, by the names Signal's list gives them ([Signal](signal.md#calls)) |
| Kinds | one-to-one and group calls, `AUDIO` (sound alone) or `VIDEO` (sound and this side's picture) |
| Rings | a call whose offer reaches the R1 within 60 s of the service taking it, which needs Signal's socket to stand ([Power](#power)) |
| Sound | Opus, 48 kHz, one channel, a packet of 240 bytes every 60 ms, 32 kbit/s, three CELT frames at wideband; the echo canceller and a jitter buffer for each far member ([Media](../firmware/media.md#a-calls-sound)) |
| Pictures | VP8 sent and offered, 640x480, each a whole frame of the camera's high speed stream; the far side's, VP8 or in a group's call VP9, decoded on a thread of their own and made as large as the screen's room takes them ([Pictures](#pictures)) |
| Way | ICE as either role; Signal's relays over UDP, TCP and TLS; a group's call through the calling server ([The way](#the-way)) |
| Keys | SRTP over AES-256-GCM one-to-one and AES-128-GCM in groups, from an X25519 agreement; a group's frames encrypted for its members ([Media](#media), [Group calls](#group-calls)) |
| Rules | RingRTC's and Signal Desktop's, each constant naming its source where it is defined; where the course departs from RingRTC, the code says why ([A one-to-one call](#a-one-to-one-call)) |
| Code | `r1_funcore::domain::calls` (the course of each kind of call, the machine over both, the board, the pictures), `r1_funcore::signal::call` (the call messages, the words over a one-to-one call's way, a group's frame encryption, what devices and the calling server tell each other), `r1_funcore::net::{stun, ice, turn, rtp}`, `r1_funcore::codec::{opus, vp8, vp9}`, `r1_funcore::hw::sound::{call, echo, jitter}`, `crates/impshell/src/calls/` (the threads and their sockets) |

## The parts

| Thread | Does | Code |
|---|---|---|
| `calls` | carries the call: does the steps of one `domain::calls::Machine`, one effect each (`Machine::next`, `Step`), waits on its nudge, the end of the stay its hold made, the microphone's word that a packet waits and the agent's sockets, at most until the machine's next time (`Step::Wait`) | `crates/impshell/src/calls/mod.rs` |
| `call pictures` and a crew of three | codes this side's picture for the far side by the VP8 encoder's steps (`Encoder::code_steps`), on the large cores ([Pictures](#pictures)) | `calls/pictures.rs` |
| `far pictures` | decodes the far side's frames in order and makes each one's picture for the screen, on the small cores; hands the picture to the main loop, and a frame that did not decode to the `calls` thread, which asks its sender for a key frame; lets go of its decoders once no call stands ([Pictures](#pictures)) | `calls/far.rs` |
| `call requests` | asks the calling server one request each (`Step::Fetch`), under a share of the call's hold (`Hold::share`), by `net::web::ask_request`, and answers on the board; with no stay it answers none at once | `calls/mod.rs` (`Worker::fetch`) |
| `microphone`, `speaker` | record and play the call's sound from `Step::Sound(true)` to `Step::Sound(false)`, each on its own side (`hw::sound::call::Microphone`, `Speaker`), taking the lock of the sound they share with the `calls` thread (`hw::sound::call::Sound`) only to move what passes between them, so that none waits on another's work or on the board; the microphone's thread wakes the `calls` thread where a packet waits ([Media](../firmware/media.md#a-calls-sound)) | `calls/mod.rs` (`Worker::start_sound`), `sound/mod.rs` |
| `camera` | makes a picture for the far side of each frame the viewfinder draws while the calls' part wants them, 30 a second at the most ([Camera](../firmware/camera.md#a-calls-picture)) | `camera/call.rs` |

How each thread is scheduled, and on which cores: [System](../firmware/system.md#the-app).

The calls' threads and the main loop share one `domain::calls::state::State` on a `sync::Board` ([Architecture](../architecture.md#parts-and-the-main-loop)). The main loop hands it the owner's orders (`Calls::ask`, `domain::calls::Order`), what Signal's thread has for the call layer (`Calls::take_news`: the call messages that passed the gate, the relays, a group's token and members, the signals not sent) and the room the far side's picture is fitted to, which the next frame decoded takes (`Calls::want`); the `calls` thread leaves its own answers there (the stay's addresses, `Answer::Addresses`; the calling server's, `Answer::Fetched`). The report (`Calls::latest`) carries what the call layer asks of Signal's thread, in order, the call as its screen shows it (`domain::calls::View`) where that changed, and whether the far side's newest picture came, which taking the report swaps into the screen's (`Ui::far_picture_mut`). The main loop swaps the camera's newest picture in (`Calls::add_picture`), in the place of one that still waits, which is never coded and is counted for the log; the `call pictures` thread takes the newest once it is free, at the rate and the key frame's wish the `calls` thread leaves at each wait (`Machine::leave`, which leaves all the thread has for the others and says who has news of it; `State::set_video`, `State::next_picture`), and hands its frame back with how long its coding and each of its steps took (`State::add_frame`).

At each wait the `calls` thread leaves the far side's frames the machine put together for the `far pictures` thread (`Machine::leave`, `State::add_arrivals`), 8 waiting at the most (`MOST_WAITING`, libwebrtc's `kZeroPlayoutDelayMaxDecodeQueueSize`): a frame that finds 8 waiting, of whichever senders, has them dropped, and is dropped too unless it is a key frame. Each sender whose frames were dropped, the frame's own too but where it is a key frame, has its stream want a key frame, once (`Machine::want_keys`, `Session::want_key`), which a PLI asks for and asks again until a key frame is handed over ([Network](../firmware/network.md#stun-ice-turn-and-rtp)); and each such sender's frames are dropped until its own key frame comes, another sender's key frame letting none of them through. The `far pictures` thread takes them in order with the room and the screen's pixel words (`State::next_arrival`, `Decoding`), and leaves what came of each (`State::add_shown`): its picture, swapped onto the board for the report, the thread taking back the picture there to make the next in, or its sender for the `calls` thread, which asks that sender for a key frame as it next takes what the board holds.

The machine owns one call at a time, a one-to-one call's course (`direct.rs`) or a group's (`group.rs`), each a value without its transport whose steps it carries out: what goes to Signal (`Command::Signal`, `Command::Course` for the log, `Command::Relays`, `Command::Token`), the agent of the call's ways, the session of each way, the sound, this side's frames, and the far side's frames, put together and opened, for their decoder. Every step's time is the paced clock's, and the log's notes take the wall clock's milliseconds. Its library is one type that is Signal's, STUN's and SRTP's at once (`domain::calls::Library`), impshell's `crypto::Library` over AWS-LC.

## A one-to-one call

`domain::calls::direct::Call` is RingRTC's `Call` with a `Connection` for each device of the far side that answered, as one value:

- **Begun here.** The owner's order (`Order::Call`) draws the call's id, 8 random bytes never nought (Signal's apps drop a call of id nought), asks Signal for the relays (`Command::Relays`), holds the network and reads the stay's addresses. Once the three are in, the machine makes the ICE agent, controlling, and an X25519 key pair, and the offer goes to every device of the callee's with this side's parameters (RingRTC's `ConnectionParametersV4`): the public key, the agent's user fragment and password, the most this side wants sent to it, 300 kbit/s (`MAX_BITRATE`, RingRTC's low data mode, as the screen shows a far picture small and the modem's connection may carry it), and VP8 alone as what it encodes and decodes. A call to a group is a group's call ([Group calls](#group-calls)).
- **Come in.** An offer that passed the gate begins a call where none stands, with the sender's identity key and the account's own as the gate hands them, the relays asked and the network held as for one begun here. Once they are in, the agent is made controlled, the answer goes to the caller's device alone, its way opens with the offer's parameters, and the candidates that came meanwhile are taken. Nothing shows before the call rings.
- **The ways.** Each device of the callee's that answers gets a side of its own at the agent (`Agent::far_credentials`, by its number) and a session of its own under the keys drawn with it ([Media](#media)); a device's second answer, and an answer once a device took the call, open nothing. The candidates this side finds over one look at the agent go as one signal, to every device of a callee's or to the caller's device.
- **Rings.** The first way to stand has the call ring, there (`Phase::Ringing`) or here (`Phase::Incoming`), and the log is told so once; a call that came in can be taken only then. While the key's window may not be left, which no ring's window rises over, a call rings at the owner's other devices alone: here it neither sounds, lights the screen nor wakes the device (`Ui::does_call_ring`), and it is not sent away, as that would end its ring there too.
- **Taken.** The owner's `ANSWER` (`Order::Accept`) tells the caller over the way, in one word, that the call is taken and what this side sends, with its picture where the offer was of a video call; a caller told by a device's word that it took the call has that device the one the call is with, tells the others which device took it, over their ways and through the service (`Hangup` `ACCEPTED`), and lets go of their ways. From then on what the far side told meanwhile counts, sound and pictures flow over that device's way, and the word of what this side sends goes again every second (`RETELL_EVERY`, RingRTC's tick), a change at once with its sequence number one higher.
- **Over.** A call not talked in 60 s after the click or the offer's arrival ends (`SETUP_TIME`, RingRTC's `TIME_OUT_PERIOD`): `NOT ANSWERED`, or failed where the far side took it and the way never stood.
- **Reconnects.** A way that loses consent while the call is talked in has the call `RECONNECTING` and its ways sought anew from the stay's addresses (`Effect::Regather`); one that stands again talks on. A way lost for good, of a device of several before one took the call, is let go of; the way the call is with, or the last, fails the call.

| Ends by | This side sends | The screen tells | The log |
|---|---|---|---|
| the owner, by a click, a swipe or leaving the screen | a plain hang-up over every way, and through the service unless the far side never heard of the call | `ENDED` | hung up here |
| a device of the callee's that sends a plain hang-up before the call is taken | that device's decline to the callee's other devices, over their ways and through the service | `DECLINED` | the far side hung up |
| a device of the callee's that is busy | its busy to the other devices, the same way | `BUSY` | missed |
| a callee that takes no calls of the account before it accepts its request | its word to the other devices, the same way | `NOT ACCEPTED YET` | missed |
| the far side, once the call is taken | nothing | `HUNG UP` | the far side hung up |
| another device of the owner's that took the call or sent it away | nothing | `ON ANOTHER DEVICE` | accepted, or missed |
| 60 s without talking | a plain hang-up, as the owner's | `NOT ANSWERED` | missed |
| a failure here: no stay, no keys, a signal the service did not take, a way lost | a plain hang-up, but by a call that came in and was not taken yet, so that the caller's ring goes on at the owner's other devices (RingRTC's `should_send_hangup_on_failure`) | the failure's word | missed |

A hang-up that names this device is its own doing, passed on, and changes nothing; so does one from a device the call is not with.

**An offer while a call stands** (RingRTC's `check_for_collision`): from another account, or from another device than the call is with, it is answered busy and logged missed; from the account a call not talked in yet is with, the call of the higher id stays and the other's ends with a hang-up, both ending where the ids are equal and the offer answered busy; from the device a call talked in is with, that call ends without a word and the offer's begins. An offer while a group's call stands, or rings, is answered busy.

**The words over the way** (`signal::call::data::Message`, RingRTC's `rtp_data.proto`): that the call is taken, a hang-up, what a side sends of itself (its picture, its sound) and the most it wants sent to it, each with the call's id, and a sequence number one higher at every change; what a side sends and the most it wants count only where the word's number is newer than the last taken of them. They go as RTP of payload type 101 on SSRC `0xD`, its sequence number and timestamp one counter from 1, unpaced.

**Where the course departs from RingRTC**, as `direct.rs` states each:

- Candidates of a device that has not answered are passed over, where RingRTC ends the call: its clients send the answer first.
- Once a device took the call, another's busy changes nothing.
- A signal the service did not take ends the call but for candidates once a way stands and a hang-up, where RingRTC ends the call for a hang-up it passed on too.

## The way

- **The agent.** One `net::ice::Agent` for the call ([Network](../firmware/network.md#stun-ice-turn-and-rtp)): controlling for a call begun here and toward the calling server, controlled for a call that came in. It is given the stay's addresses (`dial::local_addresses`: the interface's IPv4 address and a global IPv6 one), on each of which it has a UDP socket bound and an active TCP candidate. The agent is never made relayed: every call offers this side's host and server-reflexive candidates.
- **Relays.** Each relay Signal's service hands out ([Signal](signal.md#calls)) stands for TURN servers by the addresses it names (`urls_with_ips`), so that no name is looked up: each `turn` or `turns` URI a server (`turn::Server`), checked by the relay's host name under TLS, its credential running out at the time it was had and its time to live, never where it names none; each `stun` URI over UDP a STUN server (`Agent::add_stun`). The relays come at the click and at an offer, and the call goes on once they came or none could be had.
- **Streams.** A relay or the calling server reached over TCP is reached from the address the kernel picks for the stay, under TLS of `Purpose::Relay` where a name is given: Mozilla's roots, TLS 1.3 and 1.2, no ALPN ([Network](../firmware/network.md#connections-and-tls)).
- **The stay.** The call holds the network for itself ([Power](#power)); the stay's end (`NetworkDown`) has it hold anew while the call stands, and the new stay's addresses take the place of the old one's at the agent (`Agent::drop_local`, `Agent::local`), from which the ways are found anew.
- **A group's way.** The calling server is the agent's one far side, under the credentials the join's answer names; its UDP, TCP and TLS addresses stand as its candidates (`Agent::far_server`), the TLS ones checked by its host name. The candidates this side finds are told nobody: the server learns this side's addresses from its checks.

## Media

Each way's media is one `net::rtp::session::Session`: RTP and RTCP over one transport, SRTP, the streams this side sends and those it takes, their feedback and the send rate ([Network](../firmware/network.md#stun-ice-turn-and-rtp)). ICE's datagrams and the session's are told apart by their first byte.

**Keys.** A one-to-one call's (`signal::call::negotiate`, RingRTC's `negotiate_srtp_keys`): X25519 of this side's pair and the far side's public key, a key that agrees on nothing refused; HKDF-SHA256 under a salt of 32 noughts, its info `Signal_Calling_20200807_SignallingDH_SRTPKey_KDF` with the caller's and then the callee's identity key behind it, as the gate handed them; 88 bytes: the offer's key and salt (32 and 12), then the answer's. The suite is `AEAD_AES_256_GCM`; the caller sends under the offer's, the callee under the answer's. A group's call's (`sfu::negotiate`): X25519 with the server's public key, HKDF-SHA256 without salt under `Signal_Group_Call_20211105_SignallingDH_SRTPKey_KDF` alone, 56 bytes: the client's key and salt (16 and 12), then the server's; `AEAD_AES_128_GCM`.

| Streams | One-to-one | Group |
|---|---|---|
| SSRCs this side sends on | the caller's 1000, the callee's 2000, and above it: sound 2, picture 3, the picture sent again 13; data `0xD` either way | its demux id D, whose lowest four bits are nought: sound D, picture D+2, sent again D+3, data through the server D+0xD; to and from the server 1; a member's VP9 comes on its D+8, sent again on D+9 |
| Far streams a session takes | 4 (`FAR_STREAMS`) | 4 for each member of the group, for 8 members at the least (`sfu::CALL_DEVICES`, the server's default of devices a call) |
| Payload types | data 101, Opus 102, VP8 108 and its RTX 118, RED 120 and its RTX 121, ULPFEC 122 (dropped) | data 101, Opus 102, VP8 108 and its RTX 118, a member's VP9 109 and its RTX 119; the server's padding, 99, passed over |

**Sound.** Each Opus packet of the microphone's ([Media](../firmware/media.md#a-calls-sound)) goes at once, the stream's first marked, at the 48 kHz RTP clock, with no header extension one-to-one and with its level (RFC 6464) in a group's call, which the server reads. What comes goes to the speaker's jitter buffer of its member (`Step::Hear`): of the device the call is with in a one-to-one call, of every device in a group's.

**This side's picture** (`Session::send_video`): each frame cut into packets of at most 1200 bytes less the 2 RTX adds, of nearly equal size (libwebrtc's `SplitAboutEqually`), at the 90 kHz clock, each with a transport-wide sequence number, the last of a key frame, of a frame turned anew or turned at all with its orientation. One-to-one it goes as plain VP8 under a descriptor of 4 bytes whose picture id of 15 bits counts frames from a random start; in a group's call under the descriptor's one byte, with the dependency descriptor on every packet, its structure of one layer on a key frame's first, and the video layers allocation on a key frame's first and where the rate changed. The pacer refuses a frame while its queue would take longer than 2 s, and after a frame that was not sent none goes until a key frame does (`Machine::take_frames`).

**The far side's picture.** VP8 plain or in RED (its primary block), sent again as RTX, is put together into frames: one-to-one by its picture id, of the device the call is with alone; in a group's call by the dependency descriptor, of the device shown alone ([Group calls](#group-calls)). A member whose app sends spatial layers sends VP9: each layer's frame of a picture comes as a frame of its own, is opened by itself under its sender's key, and the layers are joined lower first for the decoder (`rtp::frames::Frame::open`, `Frame::join`); a frame that does not open has a key frame asked of its sender. Each frame goes to the `far pictures` thread to be decoded ([The parts](#the-parts), [Pictures](#pictures)).

**Feedback.** Transport-wide feedback for every packet that carries a number, NACKs for missing video, a PLI where a far stream wants a key frame, and reports ([Network](../firmware/network.md#stun-ice-turn-and-rtp)). A NACK of this side's video is answered as RTX from what was sent in the last 3 s; a PLI or FIR asks this side's encoder for a key frame. What is missing of a far stream goes by its packets' own sequence numbers, extended, whatever the count of its reports does at a jump (RFC 3550, A.1). The session asks for a look on the clock only where a pass has work (`Session::next_time`): the NACKs' while the far side sends pictures, the feedback's while arrivals wait to be fed back, the send rate's once this side has sent pictures; in a call of sound alone the `calls` thread wakes for the reports, the packets and its sockets.

**The send rate** follows the transport-wide feedback within the call's bounds, and the whole of it is the picture's: sound carries no transport-wide number in Signal's calls, so libwebrtc counts none of it (`Session::video_rate`).

| Bounds | One-to-one | Group |
|---|---|---|
| least | 30 kbit/s (RingRTC's `MIN_SEND_RATE`) | 30 kbit/s |
| first | 300 kbit/s, libwebrtc's factory's | 300 kbit/s |
| most | 2 Mbit/s; 1 Mbit/s where the selected pair goes through a relay (`RELAYED_MAX_SEND_RATE`); less where the far side wants less, by its parameters, then by its word over the way | 1 Mbit/s with fewer than 8 other devices in the call, 671 kbit/s with 8 or more (RingRTC's `SMALL_CALL_MAX_SEND_RATE`, `LARGE_CALL_MAX_SEND_RATE`); nothing while no other device is in the call |

**Key frames** are this side's at the picture's start, at the far side's PLI or FIR and after a frame that was not sent (`Machine::take_key_wish`); the encoder's first frame is one whatever is asked.

## Pictures

**This side's.** A video call's own picture is a photo viewfinder that calls, and the camera makes a picture of 640x480 for the far side of each frame it draws while this side's picture goes, 30 a second at the most ([Camera](../firmware/camera.md#a-calls-picture)). The main loop tells the camera at each pass whether the calls' part wants them (`Calls::wants_picture`, `Camera::want_call_pictures`) and moves each picture the camera made to the calls' board by a swap (`Camera::take_call_picture`, `Calls::add_picture`). A newer picture takes the place of one that still waits, which is never coded and is counted in the log (`State::add_picture`); the `call pictures` thread takes the newest once it is free, so that the picture it codes is never older than a frame.

- **The encoder** (`domain::calls::picture::Sender` over `codec::vp8::Encoder`, [Media](../firmware/media.md#vp8)) runs its six steps in order (`Encoder::code_steps`), each step's work on the thread's crew (`Crew::run`): parts at once, or the coding's and the ends' waves of macroblock rows ([Media](../firmware/media.md#vp8)), on the large cores ([System](../firmware/system.md#the-app)). Where no picture goes any more, the thread lets go of its encoder (`Coding::Rest`).
- **The rate** is the session's target. The quantizer of each frame is libvpx's one-pass rate control in outline (`ratectrl.c`, `vp8_update_rate_correction_factors`): a frame's bits taken to fall as its quantizer's step grows, by a factor corrected after every frame by a quarter of what the frame showed (`CORRECTION`, the limit of a frame coded once in `onyx_if.c`), a key frame by a factor of its own; a frame nothing is known for takes the middle of the scale (`FIRST_QUANTIZER`, 64). A key frame may take what the rate carries in 300 ms (`KEY_SPAN`, libwebrtc's `MaxIntraTarget` for libvpx). No frame is skipped for the rate.
- **What the far side is told** of a frame: its size, its time, the frames a second the time since the one before makes, at most the stream's 30, and its orientation: the quarter turns that stand the picture upright as the device is held and whether the rear camera looks, the flip never, as the picture goes as the sensor has it, unturned.

**The far side's** (`domain::calls::picture::Pictures`, on the `far pictures` thread): a frame is decoded by its codec's decoder (`Pictures::decode`, `codec::vp8::Decoder`, `codec::vp9::Decoder`, [Media](../firmware/media.md#vp9)), and its picture is made at the size it shows at (`Decoded::show`): as large as its shape has within the room the screen names (`Ui::far_picture_room`) once turned by the quarter turns its orientation asks, grown where it comes smaller (`gfx::to_fill`), the flip passed over as libwebrtc passes it over. Each plane is brought to that size before the turn, shrunk by the mean of what each sample covers (`base::planes::Cover`) or grown by the mix of the four samples about each one's middle (`base::sampling::Place`); each pixel's word is made of them in Rec. 601's video range, VP8's colours where a stream names none (RFC 6386, 9.2) and a VP9 picture's too, whatever its header names (`Colours::row`); then the words are turned (`sampling::turn_words`). The picture is made in one the board swaps with the screen's: the newest goes onto the board, the thread taking back the one there to make the next in, and the main loop swaps it into the screen's as it takes the report (`State::add_shown`, `Calls::latest`, `Ui::far_picture_mut`), so that neither side allocates a picture for a frame. A frame that does not decode has a key frame asked of its sender (`Shown::Flaw`, `Machine::want_keys`, `Session::want_key`).

## Group calls

`domain::calls::group::Call` is RingRTC's group call `Client`, with a ring that came before it, as one value. The calling server is `https://sfu.voip.signal.org` (`sfu::SERVER`, Signal Desktop's `sfuUrl`), a call's place on it `/v2/conference/participants`.

- **Its token and members.** A call begun in a group, or a ring taken, asks Signal's thread for the group's token (`Command::Token`), which hands the call layer the token and the group's members, each by its opaque id and the name Signal's list gives it, the owner among them ([Signal](signal.md#calls)); none fails the call as no network. The token and the members are asked anew a day later (`TOKEN_AGE`).
- **The join.** `PUT` under `Authorization: Basic` of the token's first part as the user and the token as the password, with `User-Agent`, its body this side's ICE credentials (the agent's), the public key of a fresh X25519 pair in hex and no extra info (`sfu::join_request`). The answer gives this device's demux id, the server's UDP, TCP and TLS addresses with the host name of the TLS ones, its ICE credentials and public key, who began the call, the call's era and whether the device is let in (`ACTIVE`) or waits to be; one that waits is let in once a peek lists it. A 413 is `CALL IS FULL`, a server's error no answer, any other refusal refused, none no network. A redirect has the same request made again at the address its location names, read against the request's own as a fetch reads one (`http::Target::resolve`), 3 times at the most (`net::fetch::MAX_HOPS`); one that names no address under TLS fails the request as a redirect too many does. The requests go over HTTPS under Signal's roots (`web::ask_request`, [Network](../firmware/network.md#http-and-websocket)).
- **The call's id** is its era's, as RingRTC reads one ([Signal](signal.md#calls)); a ring's id is the same number.
- **Peeks** (`GET` at the same place) tell the devices in the call, each by its demux id and its member's opaque id: at once after the join, after the server tells that a device joined or left, after data or a key of a device not known, after a device's leave and again 2 s later, and whenever the last is 10 s old; one unanswered 5 s is asked again, and after one that failed none goes sooner than 5 s, as RingRTC's group call asks them (`group_call.rs`). A 404 is a call with no devices.
- **The way** is the server's alone ([The way](#the-way)); a way that never stood, or is lost for good, ends the call as no network, and one that breaks is sought anew.
- **Sound and pictures** flow while this device is let in, the way carries them and another device is in the call ([Media](#media)); the call is talked in from then on. Alone after its own ring, it rings in the group (`RINGING`).

**Frame encryption** (`signal::call::frame`, RingRTC's `core/crypto.rs`). Every frame this side sends through the server, sound, picture and data to the other devices, is sealed: AES-256-CTR from a counter block of the frame's number, 8 bytes highest first, then noughts, and the first 16 bytes of HMAC-SHA256 over that block, the ciphertext's length, the ciphertext and 4 noughts, behind it the ratchet's step (1 byte), the frame's number (4 bytes) and the MAC. The keys are drawn from a 32-byte secret by HKDF-SHA256 without salt under `RingRTC AES Key` and `RingRTC HMAC Key`, and the ratchet's next secret under `RingRTC Ratchet`. One count of frames for all this side sends, from 1; past 32 bits nothing more is sealed.

**The keys' schedule.**

- A fresh random secret at the join. Devices new in a peek have the ratchet stepped on, and the key goes to their users alone, so that they open nothing sent before; a user gone from a peek has a new random secret go at once to the users of the devices that stay, taken into use 5 s later (`ROTATION_DELAY`); another leave meanwhile has another follow once that one is in use. A user who joins meanwhile is sent the secret that waits too.
- A key (`GroupSignal::MediaKey`) goes over Signal: to the members named where more than one of the others takes it, to the one where one does, and to the account's own devices where the owner is among the users.
- A member's key is taken only from the user the last peek gives that device to; one for a device no peek told yet is held, 40 at the most (the server's default of 8 devices a call, `sfu::CALL_DEVICES`, 5 secrets each), and the members and the devices asked anew. A device's 5 newest secrets are kept, the frame's ratchet step choosing one or stepping one on, a frame up to 300 behind and 5 steps back still opened.

**What goes to the server and through it.**

- To the server, on SSRC 1: a video request every second and where what is asked changed, the device shown at a height of 382 lines (`SHOWN_HEIGHT`, the largest lesser side a far picture shows at in any theme: a square one in Rail's room of 382 by 430 above this side's picture, [Interface](interface.md#calls)), the others at none, at most 500 kbit/s (`MAX_KBPS`, RingRTC's low data mode); the acknowledgement of the server's reliable messages, each taken once and in order as RingRTC's MRP takes them (a window of 64); the leave, twice.
- From the server: who speaks and that a device joined or left; a removal ends the call as refused.
- Through the server to the devices, sealed as frames are, on D+0xD: a heartbeat every second and at each change (whether the microphone is muted and the picture sent, and the device that muted this one), and the leave. A device's ask that this one mute is followed once: the microphone is heard no more, and the others are told by whom.
- **Shown** is the server's latest speaker, else the first device in the call: its picture alone is asked for and decoded, and the screen names each device beside this one by its member's name, with whether it speaks, is muted or sends a picture.

**Rings.**

- The owner's call in a group of fewer than 16 members (Signal Desktop's `maxGroupCallRingSize`), where the join's answer names the owner as the call's creator, rings the group once, urgent (`RingIntention`); the owner's leave while alone after that ring cancels it.
- A ring that comes rings here for 60 s at the most (RingRTC's `INCOMING_GROUP_CALL_RING_TIME`), under the group's name, and holds no network. `ANSWER` tells the account's other devices (`RingResponse` `ACCEPTED`), asks the token and joins, without this side's picture; `DECLINE` tells them `DECLINED` and logs the call missed. The ringer's cancel ends the ring (`HUNG UP`), and so does another device of the owner's that answered it (`ON ANOTHER DEVICE`). A ring of another group while a call stands is answered busy to the account's own devices.
- **The log** keeps the owner's own call as an outgoing ring, another's joined as joined, and a ring's course as it rang, was taken or was missed; a leave tells it nothing.

**Leaving.** The ring cancelled where it stands, the leave through the server, over Signal to the users of the last peek and the account's own devices (`GroupSignal::Leaving`), and to the server twice; then the way is let go of.

## Power

- **The network.** A call holds the network through an `Online` of its own (`Step::Hold`): from the owner's order, or from a one-to-one offer's arrival, as its answer goes before the owner sees anything, to its end (`Step::Release`). A group's ring holds nothing until it is taken. The calling server's requests run beside the hold under a share of it.
- **Awake.** While a call stands the calls' part is not off (`Calls::is_off`, `Parts::is_quiet`), so the device does not suspend, and the interface keeps it awake ([Interface](interface.md#calls)); an audio call needs nobody watching, so its screen goes dark 15 s after the last input ([Interface](interface.md#light-and-sleep)).
- **Signal's socket** is held while the log or its choice shows on a device awake, and while a call stands (`Ui::are_calls_wanted`, `Signal::set_calling`), and with it a suspend and the radio's rest are held off ([Signal](signal.md#power)). Nothing holds it for a call that might come: an offer that comes while it does not stand waits at the service for the next fetch, and is logged missed where it is older than 60 s by then.
- **Sound.** The microphone and the speaker run from the call's talking to its end, and the card is held both ways at once ([Media](../firmware/media.md#a-calls-sound)).
- **Pictures.** The camera runs for a video call while this side's picture goes, the device is awake and no viewfinder shows on the screen, and makes pictures for the far side only while the calls' part wants them; the `call pictures` thread holds its encoder only while a picture goes, and takes the four large cores for as long as it codes one, under a floor that holds their clock at no less than the fastest step of their low voltage while it codes, and none between pictures ([System](../firmware/system.md#the-app)); the `far pictures` thread holds its decoders while a call stands.

## The log

- `calls:` lines of the thread's effects: `calls: no stay in a network: ...`, `calls: no stay for a request of the calling server`, `calls: a request of the calling server: ...`, `calls: opening a socket: ...`, `calls: beginning a stream: ...`, `calls: a stream not connected: ...`, `calls: a stream broke: ...`, `calls: a datagram not sent: ...`, `calls: reading a socket: ...`, `calls: the microphone and the speaker: ...`, `calls: the microphone's thread's priority: ...`, `calls: the speaker's thread's priority: ...`, `calls: the thread's priority: ...`, `calls: a request's scheduling: ...`, `calls: the far pictures' thread is not kept on its cores: ...`, `calls: the thread ends: ...`; of the machine's: `calls: no stay in a network for the call`, `calls: the library gave no random bytes for a call`, `calls: no keys agreed with a device of the far side`; and the pictures' thread's `call pictures: the calling thread is not kept on its core: ...` and `call pictures: the clock floor: ...`.
- The agent's `ice:` lines, of a far side's state, its selected pair, a relay that failed and a relay's answer passed over ([Network](../firmware/network.md#stun-ice-turn-and-rtp)).
- As the sound stops, the echo canceller's line, the card's line and a line of each far member's sound ([Media](../firmware/media.md#a-calls-sound)), then what the call's pictures cost (`picture::Figures`): where any was coded, `calls: N pictures coded in X ms on the mean at R kbit/s asked and quantizer Q on the mean, B bytes a second, K key frames of them in X ms at the longest and the others in Y ms at the longest, M more made by the camera and replaced by a newer one before they were coded`, its bytes a second those of every frame after the first over the picture time from the first to the last, and `calls: the codings' steps on the mean: interpolations I us, bands B us, code C us, counts N us, probabilities P us, ends E us`; and `calls: N far frames decoded in X ms on the mean and Y ms at the longest, M pictures made of them in X ms on the mean and Y ms at the longest, at most K frames waiting to be decoded` where any was decoded.
- A system snapshot as the sound starts and one as it stops (`call talked in`, `call over`), which together tell what the call's threads took of the cores ([Logs](../firmware/logs.md#daily-files)).
- A request of the calling server's connection, as every connection is logged (`net::to_web_line`).
- Signal's line of a call's signal not sent ([Signal](signal.md#calls)), and the bell's `bell: rings` and `bell: silent` as a call's ring plays and stops.

No line tells with whom a call is, a name, a key or a password: an order, a view, a member, a signal, the parameters, a server's credential and the calling server's answers are told without them wherever they are formatted (`Debug`).

## Files

The call log is Signal's file `calls` ([Signal](signal.md#files)). Nothing else of a call is kept: the relays, a group's token, the keys, the sound and the pictures are held in memory alone, and nothing of a call is recorded.

## Not there

- No ringback tone: the caller's screen tells `RINGING`, and nothing plays.
- No switch of this side's picture during a call: a `VIDEO` call sends it from its start, an answered one-to-one video call from the answer, and an `AUDIO` call or a group's ring taken never (the screens give no `Order::Video`).
- No screen sharing, no call links (a log's sync of a call by a link is passed over), and no reactions or raised hands in a group's call.
- No calls through relays alone: the far side may learn the device's addresses.
- VP8 alone is sent and offered, one layer in a group's call; VP9 is decoded in a group's call and never sent; no H.265.
- Of a group's call, no `GroupCallUpdate` goes to the group and no statistics go to the server.
