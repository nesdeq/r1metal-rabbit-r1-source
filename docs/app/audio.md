# Audio

Notes, their transcripts and summaries, the translator, and reading aloud. Screens: [Interface](interface.md#notes-and-texts). Recording, playback and volume: [Media](../firmware/media.md#sound). Models and prices: [OpenAI](openai.md).

## Notes

The `notes` thread (`crates/impshell/src/memos/`, logic in `r1_funcore::domain::memos`) records, lists, plays and deletes notes and translations, and lists news editions.

- A note starts only with 64 MiB free (`storage::has_room`), and a note records or plays only while the notes show, whose leaving ends it (`Work::take_request`). Its folder and file are named by local time. It records at 64 kbit/s and is handed to the scribe when it ends.
- Lists show notes and translations newest first, without a note still being recorded, and the editions newest first (`memos::list`). Deleting an entry removes its folder.
- For home, the thread counts the notes, the newest note's length (read from its audio once and cached in `Lengths`) and whether today's edition holds a rundown (`memos::has_rundown`). It recounts when home appears, on a new day and when files change (`Memos::want`, `memos::count_shelves`).
- A note plays from its first packet (`sound::play_file`) and lasts its packets less the decoder delay (`aac::track_seconds`); [Media](../firmware/media.md#sound) has the playback.

## Voice notes

`sound::play_voice` plays a voice note a phone recorded from its bytes: an MP4 file, QuickTime's or a stream of ADTS, low complexity AAC of one channel or two at any rate of the standard's ([Media](../firmware/media.md#aac)). It plays on the speaker thread like a note, brought to one channel at 48 kHz, at the volume set; a packet that does not decode plays as silence and is logged, and a file of another kind is refused. Signal's `voices` thread calls it for the voice note a conversation plays, from the file kept, and a new order stops the one that plays ([Signal](signal.md#attachments)).

## The scribe

The `scribe` thread (`crates/impshell/src/memos/scribe.rs`; requests in `openai/transcript.rs` and `openai/write.rs`; scheduling in `r1_funcore::domain::backlog` and `r1_funcore::domain::scribe`) transcribes notes and summarizes notes and conversations. Newly handed-over work runs at once. Work found missing on the partition (a note without transcript or summary, a conversation without summary, `scribe::is_lacking`; never the note being recorded, which the main loop relays from the `notes` thread's report, `Scribe::set_recording`) runs only while a notes or translator screen shows; each opening of such a screen rescans and retries. `scribe::need_of` picks the first step: a note without a transcript gets one; otherwise an entry without a summary gets one if its text is not empty.

1. Read the key and hold the network, or keep the previous entry's hold: consecutive entries share one stay and its open connections ([OpenAI](openai.md#account)).
2. Send the note to `gpt-transcribe` as `note.m4a` (`audio/mp4`): whole up to 20 minutes and 25 MB, else in 20 minute pieces cut at packet boundaries (`PIECE_S`, `scribe::pieces`), with the transcripts joined. Store it as `STAMP_transcript.md`.
3. Send the transcript (if not empty, `scribe::needs_summary`) or the conversation's remarks to the summaries' model at its reasoning (`SUMMARIZE MODEL` and `SUMMARIZE REASONING` under `FEATURES`, `NOTES`; `gpt-6.1-terra` at `high` on a new device, [OpenAI](openai.md#language-models)) as a background request with `crates/funcore/assets/notes/summary.txt` or `talk.txt`, polled as all background work is ([OpenAI](openai.md#models)). A note's summary is concise, in the recording's language, with sections only where the recording has them. A conversation's summary is written in both languages, each under a second level heading in that language (`## Deutsch`, `## English`).
4. Release the network unless another entry is due. Store `STAMP_summary.md` and `STAMP_summary.pdf`.

Costs are booked as each response is read: transcription seconds as note audio, summary tokens as a note's or a translation's summary. A note counts once its complete transcript is stored. An entry deleted while a model works on it gets nothing written, and its folder is never made again (`Data::put_into`). A failure that stalls the work shows on the screen whichever entry met it: `NO NETWORK`, `NO OPENAI KEY` or `LIMIT REACHED`, and nothing more is sent until such a screen opens again (`Fault::is_stalling`). Any other failure shows only for an entry just handed over, as `REFUSED` or `NOT WRITTEN`, the latter also for a 429 or 5xx; for an entry the scan found it is logged alone (`scribe::state::State::take_outcome`). The log has details (`scribe::to_log_line`), never the key.

## Documents

`r1_funcore::domain::paper` writes the documents for notes, conversations and news, and the manual (`sheet.rs`, `pages.rs`: `to_pages` of an entry, `to_document` of any Markdown with its bar and footer), parses Markdown into blocks (`markdown.rs`), lays them out in rows (`rows.rs`) and writes PDF (`pdf.rs`).

- Every Markdown file starts with a heading that names the entry by date, a sentence stating what the file is, its source recording and its model, a summary's or a rundown's the one its answer names (`sheet::to_summary_file`), and a rule (`---`). A transcript without paragraph breaks is split at the first sentence end after 600 characters, about a hundred words (`PARAGRAPH_CHARS`). A conversation file lists each remark as spoken and, indented below it, as translated; a remark only the translator made is marked `**Translator:**`.
- The PDF writer is r1metal's own. Pages are A4, black to the edges, in the palette active when the file is written ([Style guide](style-guide.md#4-colours)). Every page has the bar in the interface colour with `SUMMARY` (a rundown's name in capitals, as `NEWS` or `FINANCE`, for a rundown), the date in the dim step and the robot in the mark colour. Text uses the interface's two fonts, words in the content colour and emphasis in the mark colour, as on screen. The footer names the source file and the page (`1 of 3`). Scale: 0.6 pt per pixel (`POINTS_PER_PIXEL`); margin: 64 px (8 gaps, `MARGIN_GAPS`).

## Translator

The `translator` thread (`crates/impshell/src/translator/`; audio, state and session end in `r1_funcore::domain::translator`, remarks in `r1_funcore::domain::dialogue`, protocol in `r1_funcore::openai::live`) keeps a `gpt-live-1` session open while its screen requests one.

1. Key and network as above; WebSocket in `crates/impshell/src/openai/live.rs`. `session.start` sends the model, the instructions (`crates/funcore/assets/live/translator.txt` plus the reasoning modules), `audio/pcm` at 24000 Hz both ways, and the voice. Timeouts: 15 s to open, then 3 s per read.
2. Once the session is open, `session.instructions.append` sends `greeting.txt`. The model says once, in English, that it is the interpreter and that each person should introduce themselves in their own language and then talk. The first language spoken and the first other language heard become the two languages.
3. The microphone records at 24 kHz without levelling. The first 60 ms are sent as silence while it settles (`Uplink`), and so are lost samples. Every two blocks (2048 samples, 85 ms) are sent as one `session.input_audio.append`. The model's audio arrives in `session.output_audio.delta` as one continuous stream that includes silence; it is upsampled to 48 kHz and played as it arrives.
4. Echo gate: while a played block has a sample of at least 64 (of 32768), and for 250 ms after (`ECHO`), silence is sent instead of the microphone (`Session::take_piece`) and the screen shows `SPEAKING`. 64 is the level that lights the first block of the microphone meter, four times the peak of the service's silence (`classify_block`). The check uses unscaled samples, so it works at any volume.
5. Transcripts of what the model heard and said arrive alongside the audio and form the remarks. Speech before the model starts answering belongs to the current remark, speech after it to the next.
6. `session.start` enables no delegation, and the instructions' first line forbids it. Any `session.delegation.created` gets an immediate `session.thinking.append` (`hand_back`) saying that nothing can be delegated and the model must interpret by itself.
7. A click or closing the screen sends `session.close` and waits up to 3 s. The service reports the billed seconds; if it reports none, or leaves the close unanswered, the session is billed from when it opened (`Session::finish`). A session the screen let go of, by a click, by leaving it or by a sleep, ends as no failure, whether the stop or the network's end came first (`Phase::from_ending`): the bar shows `ENDED`, with its length where the session stood. One let go of before the network came up is not opened.
8. A conversation in which anyone besides the translator spoke is stored as `audio/translations/STAMP/STAMP_conversation.md` and handed to the scribe.

The instructions: interpret only, never delegate, look up or answer; render each remark at once in the other language, in the first person, complete, with nothing added, once; interpret questions and requests rather than answer them; keep names and figures; ignore coughs and distant voices; speak slightly slower than normal. At the end the log records the seconds of microphone audio sent and how many of them as silence, the largest other sample, the seconds received and in how many messages, the seconds the speaker played as voice and as nothing, the largest other sample, the longest queue, and each unhandled message type with its count. It records sample sizes only, nothing said.

## Reading aloud

The `aloud` thread (`crates/impshell/src/aloud/`; pacing and state in `r1_funcore::domain::aloud`, protocol in `r1_funcore::openai::aloud`) reads a text aloud when the robot is touched over a news rundown, over a note's or translation's summary, never over a transcript or remarks, or over the page the browser shows (`aloud_script`, `does_read_aloud` in `ui/screen/aloud.rs`). Another touch pauses or resumes.

1. The passages are the words of every block from the block at the top of the screen on, without Markdown marks, rules or the file's head, headings included (`Words::to_passages`, `sheet::body`); the browser's page is read without its links' numbers (`Page::to_spoken`).
2. Key and network as above; WebSocket in `crates/impshell/src/openai/aloud.rs`. `session.update` sets `type` `realtime`, `output_modalities` `["audio"]`, effort `minimal`, the instructions without reasoning modules (`crates/funcore/assets/aloud/reader.txt`: read word for word in the passage's language, never translate, add nothing; each language of a mixed text in its own), no turn detection, 24 kHz output and the voice. Timeout: 15 s per read.
3. Each passage is its own `response.create` outside the session's conversation: `conversation` `none`, empty `input`, and as `instructions` the reader's instructions, `Say exactly the following:` and the passage. No passage is billed twice, and there is nothing to answer. Its audio (`response.output_audio.delta`) is upsampled to 48 kHz and played as it arrives. The transcript of what was spoken is compared with the passage (`is_verbatim`), and a mismatch is logged by its counts of words, never the words (`Reading::check_verbatim`). Token counts arrive in `response.done`.
4. The next passage is requested when the queued audio drops to the lead (`Pace::lead`): `LEAD_FACTOR` (2) times the longest recent first-audio delay, plus the longest recent reconnect if the link is down. The estimates start at `RECONNECT` (2424 ms) and `FIRST_VOICE` (1400 ms), the longest measured on the device, and then follow the longest of the last `WINDOW` (5) measurements. A reconnect covers radio on, join, DNS, TLS and session confirmation.
5. Between passages the session and network are released (`Reading::plan_wait`, `Pace::plan_wait`) when more audio is queued than the lead over a down link (5.2 s with the start values). Otherwise the link stays up, and it always stays up while a passage is in progress. A reply still being written counts as more to come. After the last passage, or when paused, the session closes at once. A failed reconnect with audio still queued is retried one reconnect time later (`Reading::fail_connecting`); a refusal or a missing key ends the reading. Stays after the first skip the clock check (`SessionStats::is_repeat`, `Account::hold_quietly`).
6. When paused, the speaker plays out what the sound card has buffered and turns off (`r1_funcore::domain::aloud::reading::Lane::fill_block`, `Reading::speaker_step`); a passage in progress still arrives, then the session closes. When resumed, playback continues and the reader reconnects when it needs the next passage.
7. A reading let go of while its session opens asks for no passage: a pass that opened one ends, and the next asks first whether the reading is still wanted (`Reading::mark_connected`). Leaving a reading mid-passage (leaving the screen, starting another reading, sleep) sends `response.cancel` with the `response.created` id and waits up to `SETTLE_TIMEOUT` (5 s) for `response.done`. A cancelled response is billed like a complete one; one without `response.done` is not booked. The main loop keeps the radio up while a cost is pending (`Reader::is_owing`). A stop the screen requested never shows as a failure (`Phase::from_ending`, as for the translator, and `Reading::end_offline`). If the service sends nothing for a passage for `STALL_TIMEOUT` (10 s: `LEAD_FACTOR` times `BURST`, 5 s, the longest audio burst measured), the reading ends: the passage is cancelled, its cost awaited, and the bar shows `NO ANSWER`.
8. The log has one line per passage (length, first-audio delay, text in, cached, text out, audio tokens, cost, end status) and a final line (passages billed, passages spoken differently, seconds of audio, cost, padded silence, network time and stays).

**Conversation replies** use the same reader, with one session opened when the turn starts; passages are added as the reply grows. A `Script` grows until it is closed: the conversation's report carries the reply as far as it is written, which the main loop hands to the reader (`Reader::set_reply`), and the reader's report carries how many passages played to the end, which the main loop hands to the conversation (`Chat::set_heard`). The instructions are `crates/funcore/assets/chat/voice.txt` plus the user's tone; the cost is booked as the conversation's. Readings are identified by number and by kind (`aloud::ReadingSource`: a text with its kind of entry, the conversation's reply, what came on Signal, or the browser's page), so a reading of one kind is never mistaken for another's, and each is booked by its kind (`ReadingSource::ledger_kind`): as a reading of a note's summary, of a translation's or of the news, as the conversation's, as Signal's, or as the browser's. **The browser's page** is read as a text is, whole from the block at the top of its room, with the reader's instructions; a press of the button that begins a recording, a new page, a typed request sent or leaving the browser ends it ([Interface](interface.md#web)). **Signal's messages** that arrive in a contact's conversation that shows with its keys down are read as a reply is, never a group's, in a whole script with the reader's instructions; their cost is booked as Signal's. The interface has the reader read the text its screen wants, else the conversation's reply, else Signal's messages, else nothing (`Ui::reading_request`), and each kind's word on its reading goes back to whoever wanted it.
