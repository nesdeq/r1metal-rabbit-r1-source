- **Test pictures**: `crates/funcore/assets/jpeg` holds one synthetic picture encoded as JPEG in seven ways by Pillow 12 (libjpeg-turbo), and libjpeg-turbo's decoding of four of them.
- **Test sound**: `crates/funcore/assets/sound` holds synthetic signals encoded by FFmpeg's AAC encoder as MP4, QuickTime and ADTS files (`mono_16000.m4a`, `mono_48000.m4a`, `mono_48000.aac`, `quicktime_16000.mov`, `stereo_22050.m4a`, `stereo_44100.m4a`, `stereo_44100.aac`), and FFmpeg's decoding of each MP4 and QuickTime file as 16 bit samples (`.pcm`).

- **Test video**: `crates/funcore/assets/video/vp8` holds four VP8 streams in IVF files, whose frames the tests hold to ffmpeg's decoding of them, and `crates/funcore/assets/video/rtp/call_vp8.rtp` ffmpeg's RTP packets of one of them (`ffmpeg -f rtp`); `crates/funcore/assets/video/vp9` holds nine VP9 streams in IVF files that libvpx 1.17.0 encoded from FFmpeg's `testsrc2` pattern, whose frames the tests hold to libvpx's decoding of them, and `video/rtp/testsrc_vp9.ivf` with `testsrc_vp9.rtp` a VP9 stream and ffmpeg's RTP packets of it.
- **exFAT**: `crates/funcore/assets/exfat/upcase.bin` is the compressed recommended up-case table from Microsoft's exFAT specification (5836 bytes, checksum `0xE619D30D`), as exfatprogs' `mkfs.exfat` writes it.

## Services

- **OpenAI**, with the owner's API key: the image, speech, language, live and realtime models ([OpenAI](app/openai.md)).
- **News**: the 47 feeds of the two rundowns a new device has, one of them a Google News search of a publisher's site; their URLs are in `crates/funcore/assets/news/news.feeds` and `finance.feeds`. The feeds of a rundown the owner adds are those OpenAI's language model finds with its web search and the device then reads ([News](app/news.md#requests)). The device fetches every feed directly.
- **Web**: the sites of the pages the owner asks the browser for. The device fetches a page directly and stores none of it; one it cannot read is written by OpenAI's language model with its web search ([Interface](app/interface.md#web)).
- **Pokédex**: PokéAPI's GraphQL service (`graphql.pokeapi.co`; its code and data by Paul Hallett and PokéAPI's contributors, `BSD-3-Clause`) for every Pokémon's names and a Pokémon's entry in a language, and the official artwork an entry names, PokéAPI's sprites on raw.githubusercontent.com. The device fetches them directly and stores none of it. Pokémon, their names, texts and artwork are Nintendo's, Creatures' and GAME FREAK's.
- **Signal**: the chat and storage services and the groups server of the owner's Signal account, as one of its linked devices ([Signal](app/signal.md)); for its calls, the relays the chat service hands out and Signal's calling server, `sfu.voip.signal.org` ([Calls](app/calls.md)).
- **DNS**: Cloudflare's DNS over TLS, for the device's own lookups and, relayed, for those of a computer it lends the modem's connection to without the tunnel; in a network of IPv6 alone, the network's own resolver, asked for its NAT64's prefix alone.
- **The tunnel**: the WireGuard peer the owner's config names, for the packets of a computer on the cable while `VPN` is on ([Network](firmware/network.md#the-tunnel)).
- **Time**: Cloudflare's time service. The UTC offset comes from `ipwho.is` over HTTPS, whose free tier takes 1,000 requests a day without a key and allows commercial use. The offsets the clock screen steps through (`OFFSETS` in `r1_funcore::base::time::offset`) are those of the IANA time zone database 2026e (public domain), which is not in the repository.
- **Battery rundown**: pings Cloudflare's `1.1.1.1` once a second while the test runs.
